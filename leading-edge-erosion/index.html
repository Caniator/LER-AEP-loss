Aerones field data

Not cosmetic.
Aerodynamic.

Rain, hail, sand and insects hit the leading edge at full tip speed, millions of times a year. As the edge roughens, drag goes up and lift goes down. The airfoil stops doing the job it was designed for, and output falls with it.

1

Pits

The coating starts to break down. Early pitting alone can raise drag by 80% and cost 3–5% of AEP in wind-tunnel models.

2

Gouges

Pits join into gouges. In wind-tunnel tests, drag rises 150–400% over the clean blade.

3

Delamination (CAT4–5)

Erosion cuts through the coating into the glass-fibre laminate. Coating repairs no longer work, and the blade itself is at risk.

CAT2 today is CAT4 in three years.

Erosion compounds. In Aerones field data, an untreated turbine goes from no erosion to CAT5 in five years, and both lost energy and repair cost climb every year.

Repair by rope access

  • Profile shaped by hand and eye; quality varies
